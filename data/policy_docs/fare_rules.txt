FARE POLICY: BASIC_ECONOMY
- Non-refundable. No refunds or travel credits will be issued upon passenger cancellation.
- Seat selection is assigned at check-in.
- Hold duration is strictly limited to 10 minutes.
- Flight schedule changes made by the airline qualify for free rebooking or travel vouchers.

FARE POLICY: FLEXIBLE
- Cancellations permitted up to 2 hours before departure.
- Passenger cancellations yield a 90% travel credit voucher (10% administrative processing fee).
- Free seat rebooking and date adjustments allowed once per ticket.
- Hold duration is 20 minutes.

FARE POLICY: REFUNDABLE
- 100% monetary refund to original payment method upon cancellation prior to departure.
- Priority check-in and complimentary seat selection in First/Business cabins.
- Full compensation eligibility under denied-boarding or airline cancellation guidelines.
- Hold duration is 30 minutes.

AIRLINE COMPENSATION AND DELAYS
- Delays > 3 hours: meal vouchers provided at airport service desks.
- Delays > 6 hours or overnight cancellations: airline-provided hotel accommodations plus complimentary rebooking.
- Involuntary denied boarding: 100% refund plus $400 statutory travel credit.
